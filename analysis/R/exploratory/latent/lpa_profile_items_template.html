The four latent profiles across the questionnaire

LPA model 1 (equal variances), four profiles. Each line is a profile's mean answer per item, items in survey order, one panel per block on its own answer scale. Answers as given, unless reverse-scoring is on: * marks items where agreeing favours prohibition; with reverse-scoring on (*R) these are flipped (8 − answer), so on every belief and behaviour item 7 is the most liberal answer. Turn profiles on and off with the buttons; hover over a point for the item and all four means.
