Respondents on the quadrant map

Each dot is a respondent: mean preferred policy over the drugs on each axis (over the drugs answered), from ban (1) to free sale (9). Respondents with no answer for either axis are left out. Dashed lines are the weighted means of all respondents on the map, in every panel; the black cross is the panel's own weighted mean. Quadrant shares and the colour summaries are weighted. Dots are jittered slightly, since a mean over six or two answers takes few values; the jitter shrinks as the axis averages over more drugs. Drag a box over dots to list those respondents below the map; click without dragging to clear. Hollow grey rings: "geen mening" on the colour item.
