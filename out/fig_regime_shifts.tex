  \caption{Older, right-wing, practically educated and rural respondents more often prefer a ban for every substance except alcohol and tobacco. Their counterparts instead favour a specialist shop for cannabis, psychedelics and MDMA, and medical or research supply for speed, cocaine and heroin. For alcohol and tobacco, right-wing and rural respondents favour a general market over a specialist shop. Each point is the difference in weighted share choosing a regime (percentage points) between the two groups named in the legend; per drug and demographic the four differences sum to zero. Practical education includes middle; rural = low or no urbanisation, urban = high or very high. Grey bars span the four demographics. Group sizes ($n$, unweighted): 50+ 714, 18–49 525; Right 394, Left 347; Practical 727, Theoretical 512; Rural 311, Urban 707. Vote: parties grouped into left, middle and right (provisional).}
